# Homing Pigeon brand

Homing Pigeon sends text-message alerts from your code or your coding agent to one phone: yours. This document describes how it looks and sounds.

## Name

Always "Homing Pigeon", two words, capitalized. Never "HP", "Pigeon", "HomingPigeon", or any category word attached to it. The package and handle are `homingpigeondev`.

## Voice

Plain, short, sentence case. Write to the person who wants a text from their code, and say what it does for them. The page answers the question people ask their coding agent, and the answer is yes.

Internal positioning stays internal. How we think about the product, what it is not, how it compares to Twilio or marketing tools, and rules we hold ourselves to belong in the brief, not on the page. Legal limits such as STOP and the delivery disclaimer live in the terms.

Humor is dry and about pigeons being slow. We make that joke once, in the tagline, and then stop. No exclamation marks. No "supercharge". No emoji in copy.

The pigeon never speaks. It appears in pictures only. It does not have a name, a voice, a speech bubble or an opinion.

## Visual identity

The site looks like a personal homepage from the late nineties: a starfield, beveled gray windows with navy title bars, one rainbow title, a pixel-art pigeon and 88×31 badges in the footer. The joke is that this is obviously not a marketing funnel. It is somebody's page about a thing they made, and the thing works.

Rules that keep it from becoming a costume:

- **Rainbow lives in two places only**: the H1 and the horizontal rules. Everything else is black, gray, navy and white.
- **Text is always readable.** Body copy is black on gray or light gray on black. The pixel typeface is for headings and the tagline, never for paragraphs.
- **Nothing is rounded.** Corners are square, borders are bevels.
- **Nothing moves except the stars**, and only when the visitor allows motion.
- **Code is lime on black**, because that is where the eye goes and that is what people copy.

### Color

Ground: Windows gray `#c0c0c0` in light, black `#000000` in dark. Windows are always gray with black text. Title bars are navy `#000080`. Code is `#5bff6a` on black. The accent is purple `#6a1fc9` on gray and yellow `#ffe14a` on black. The rainbow runs pink, yellow, green, blue, purple.

### Type

- **Press Start 2P** for the H1, section titles, tagline and badges.
- **Verdana** for everything you read.
- **Courier New** for commands and sample texts.

Two of the three are system fonts. The page loads one small font file.

### The mark

The mark is the pixel pigeon: gray, iridescent green-and-purple neck, an envelope in its beak, pink feet, with a dotted line running back to a small house. Use the full image on the home page and the share card. Use the head-and-envelope crop, on black, for icons and avatars.

Always show it at integer multiples of its pixel grid with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Never rotate, recolor, blur or add a drop shadow. Never put words inside the image.

## What we do not show

No dashboards, logins, chat windows or contact lists, because they do not exist. No testimonials, user counts or uptime badges. No third-party logos. No phone numbers. No promotional text in any sample message.
